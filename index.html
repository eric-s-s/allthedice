Dice Statistics Calculator

Not all dice rolls are created equal. If you roll two six-sided dice, your chance of rolling a 7 is 17% while your chance of rolling 2 is just 3%. This website calculates the probabilities for any combination of any kinds of dice.

Examples
Quickstart

In Inputs, type dice as <number>*<die>, for example 2*Die(6), and press Enter or Calculate. The plot shows every possible roll and its percent chance.

Join different dice with &: 2*Die(6)&1*Die(8). Add more dice collections to compare them on the same plot. Try 3*Die(6) next to BestOfDicePool(DicePool(Die(6), 4), 3) to see how much rolling four dice and keeping the best three helps.

In Stats, enter a range of rolls to see how often each collection lands in it. In Roller, roll any collection you have calculated.
